NSGA-II Results

NSGA-II (Non-dominated Sorting Genetic Algorithm II) is a popular multi-objective evolutionary algorithm. It sorts solutions by dominance and crowding distance, evolving a diverse Pareto front. NSGA-II is well-suited for problems where you want a spread of trade-off solutions.
NSGA-II uses genetic operations and non-dominated sorting to find optimal trade-offs.

MOEA/D Results

MOEA/D (Multi-Objective Evolutionary Algorithm based on Decomposition) decomposes the multi-objective problem into many single-objective subproblems and solves them in parallel. It uses reference directions to guide the search and is effective for problems with complex Pareto fronts.
MOEA/D promotes diversity by solving many subproblems simultaneously.
